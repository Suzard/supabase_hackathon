# Design context: Token Charity

- Audience: a hackathon stage audience and judges watching a live demo, plus participants
  who might donate. Read from across a room, in seconds.
- Job: see that donated credits are flowing to agents, which donor key Jev picked, and the
  moment a key runs dry and traffic moves to another.
- Tone: telethon tote board. Warm paper, oxblood board, gold for donated money, split-flap
  numerals. Charity drive, not SaaS analytics. Approved by Avi, 2026-10-03.
- Honesty rule: never show a quantity we don't have. Jars don't show remaining credit
  (unknown); they stay full while live and empty when the provider says the key is spent.
